Pass DELF A1 Exam: Your Complete Guide

Need to pass the DELF A1 exam for your French residence permit? This guide covers everything you need to know about the DELF A1 (Diplôme d'Études en Langue Française) and how to prepare effectively.

What is the DELF A1 Exam?

The DELF A1 is an official French language diploma issued by the French Ministry of Education. It certifies you can understand and use basic French expressions in everyday situations.

Who needs it:

  • Residence permit applicants in France, Switzerland, Belgium
  • Family reunification visa applicants
  • Students applying to French-speaking institutions
  • Anyone needing proof of basic French proficiency

Exam Format

The DELF A1 exam tests four competencies:

  • Compréhension orale (Listening): 20 minutes — Understand announcements and simple conversations
  • Compréhension écrite (Reading): 30 minutes — Read short texts, signs, and messages
  • Production écrite (Writing): 30 minutes — Fill out forms and write short messages
  • Production orale (Speaking): 5-7 minutes — Interview and role-play exercises
⚠️ Speaking is the hardest part: You need to speak clearly and respond naturally. Most apps don't give you real speaking practice — they just show you phrases.

How Long Does It Take to Prepare?

Typical timelines:

  • Complete beginners: 3-4 months with consistent daily study (1-2 hours/day)
  • With some French knowledge: 6-8 weeks of focused practice
  • Intensive preparation: 4-6 weeks with 3+ hours/day

What You Need to Study

Topics covered in DELF A1:

  • Introducing yourself and others
  • Describing your family, home, and daily routine
  • Shopping and asking for prices
  • Talking about hobbies and interests
  • Asking for directions
  • Making appointments

Traditional Preparation (Language Schools)

Most people prepare at Alliance Française or private language schools:

  • Cost: €1,000-2,800 for a full A1 course
  • Schedule: Fixed class times, usually 2-3 times per week
  • Duration: 8-12 weeks
  • Pros: Native teachers, structured curriculum, speaking practice
  • Cons: Very expensive, rigid schedule, group pace varies

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the DELF A1 exam cost?

Exam fees vary by country: €100-180 in France, CHF 150-220 in Switzerland. Check your local examination center for exact pricing.

Where can I take the exam?

At Alliance Française, French cultural centers, or authorized DELF examination centers. Search for "DELF exam center near me" to find your location.

What score do I need to pass?

You need 50 out of 100 points overall to pass. You must score at least 5/25 in each of the four sections (listening, reading, writing, speaking).

Is the DELF A1 certificate valid forever?

Yes, DELF diplomas are valid for life. Once you pass, you never need to retake the exam.

Can I skip A1 and take A2 directly?

Yes, DELF exams are independent. You can take any level without passing the previous ones. However, for residence permits, check which level is required.

How is DELF A1 different from TCF?

DELF is a diploma (valid forever), TCF is a test (valid 2 years). For residence permits, most countries accept both. DELF is more structured and beginner-friendly.
