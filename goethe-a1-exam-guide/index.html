Goethe A1 Exam Guide: Format, Timing & Scoring

Taking the Goethe A1 exam soon? This guide breaks down exactly what happens on test day, how each section is structured, and what you need to score to pass.

Exam Overview

The Goethe-Zertifikat A1: Start Deutsch 1 tests four language skills over approximately 80 minutes:

Section Time Points
Lesen (Reading) 25 minutes 25 points
Hören (Listening) 20 minutes 25 points
Schreiben (Writing) 20 minutes 25 points
Sprechen (Speaking) 15 minutes 25 points
Total 80 minutes 100 points

Part 1: Lesen (Reading) — 25 minutes

What you'll read:

  • Short messages and emails
  • Advertisements and notices
  • Simple instructions
  • Signs and announcements

Task types:

  • Part 1: Match short texts to situations (True/False)
  • Part 2: Match people to appropriate offers/services
  • Part 3: Read a text and answer yes/no questions

Part 2: Hören (Listening) — 20 minutes

What you'll hear:

  • Short everyday conversations
  • Announcements at train stations, airports
  • Phone messages
  • Radio announcements

Task types:

  • Part 1: Listen to 6 short recordings, answer simple questions
  • Part 2: Listen to a conversation and mark correct answers
  • Part 3: Listen to announcements and answer questions
Tip: Each listening text is played twice. Use the first listen to understand the situation, second listen to catch specific details.

Part 3: Schreiben (Writing) — 20 minutes

What you'll write:

  • Part 1: Fill out a form (name, address, date of birth, nationality, etc.)
  • Part 2: Write a short message (30-40 words) like:
    • Accepting or declining an invitation
    • Asking for information
    • Making an appointment

Part 4: Sprechen (Speaking) — 15 minutes

This is a conversation with an examiner (sometimes paired with another candidate).

Three parts:

  • Part 1 (Intro): Answer questions about yourself
    • Name, age, where you're from
    • Family, hobbies, work/study
    • Languages you speak
  • Part 2 (Questions): Ask and answer everyday questions using prompt cards
    • Topics: food, time, hobbies, family, work
    • Example: "What do you like to eat?"
  • Part 3 (Role-play): Make simple requests
    • Example: Ask a friend to help you move
    • Example: Ask someone for directions

How Scoring Works

To pass Goethe A1:

  • Overall score: 60/100 points minimum
  • Written part (Reading + Writing + Listening): minimum 45/75 points
  • Oral part (Speaking): minimum 15/25 points
Important: You must pass BOTH written and oral sections. If you only pass one, you don't get the certificate.

On Test Day

What to bring:

  • Valid ID (passport or national ID)
  • Pens (blue or black ink)
  • Exam confirmation (if provided)

What NOT to bring:

  • Phone (must be turned off and stored)
  • Dictionary
  • Notes or study materials

When Do I Get Results?

  • Results: 4-6 weeks after the exam
  • Certificate: Arrives by mail or can be picked up at the exam center

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use a dictionary during the exam?

No. No dictionaries or reference materials are allowed.

What if I'm late?

You may not be allowed to take the exam. Arrive at least 30 minutes early.

Can I retake just the speaking part if I fail?

No. You must retake the entire exam.

Is the exam the same worldwide?

Yes. The format and content are standardized across all Goethe-Institut locations.
